About Mikey’s Cards

Mikey’s Cards creates custom, randomized Pokémon card packs tailored especially for friends and fellow collectors. Every pack is put together carefully to ensure balanced collections, offering different pack sizes with clear, transparent contents so you always know what kind of card mix to expect.

Sales Information

Currently available for in-person sales only.

Packs can be collected or purchased directly in person. No online ordering or shipping is available—built simply for trading and sharing the hobby locally!
